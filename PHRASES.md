# Phrases

The 10 quick phrases, ported from the old `silent-voice-rig` repo. Recording them is in [RECORDING.md](RECORDING.md).

Lock the 10 phrases at kickoff on Friday, before the Cartesia clips and the pilot session that night.
Changing a phrase after the pilot session throws away its recordings.

## Rules

1. **Useful to the community.**
   Pick what someone in a hospital bed or at home needs to say most, like thirst, pain, and help.
2. **Different on the muscles, not just in meaning.**
   Mix tongue sounds (t, d, k, n, l, s) with lip sounds (p, b, m, f, w), since the chin pad reads the tongue.
3. **Different lengths.**
   Mix 2, 3, and 5 syllable phrases, since length alone helps the classifier tell them apart.
4. **No phrase inside another.**
   "Thank you" and "No thank you" share most of their movement, so keep only one.
5. **Opposites must look very different.**
   Jev can't separate "yes" from "no" after a question like "Want some water?", since both fit, so the muscles have to.
   Use "Yes please" and "No thank you", not "Yes" and "No".
6. **Short.**
   Each phrase must fit in one 5 s clip with 1 s of stillness on each side.

## Check before locking

1. Mouth each pair of similar phrases in front of a mirror with two fingers under the chin.
   If two feel the same, swap one out.
2. After the pilot session on Friday night, Austin checks which phrases the model mixes up.
   Swap any phrase that's often confused before session 2, then regenerate its Cartesia clip.
   A swapped phrase only gets sessions 2 and 3, so swap 2 at most.
